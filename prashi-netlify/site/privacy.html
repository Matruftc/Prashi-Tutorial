Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Prashi Tutorial handles information provided through this website, admissions forms and student portal. For privacy questions, contact the institute using the Contact and Admissions section of the home page.

1. Information we collect

Enquiry and admission forms collect the details you submit, such as your name, contact information, class, course interest and message. For enrolled students, the institute may maintain admission details, parent or guardian contacts, school details, attendance, marks, teacher remarks, doubts, enrolments, fee entries and payment receipts. An optional profile picture is stored when uploaded.

2. How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange counselling, manage admissions and classes, provide learning resources, maintain academic and fee records, and support account access. Submitting an enquiry does not itself confirm admission.

3. Access and service providers

Authorised institute personnel can access records according to their roles. Students access their own portal records and learning content made available to them. Hosting and configured email providers may process information needed to operate the service. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. The website does not include a feature for selling personal information.

4. Cookies, browser storage and external services

Essential cookies maintain sign-in sessions and protect form submissions. The browser stores your theme preference locally. Technical records may include request logs, security events and hashed network identifiers used to limit abuse. This app does not include advertising trackers.

5. Security

The app uses password hashing, server-side access checks, protected sessions, form validation and request limits. Production hosting must be configured with HTTPS. No online service can guarantee complete protection; keep your password private and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

6. Retention and deletion

Records are retained for institute administration, academic support, accounting and applicable legal requirements. This app does not automatically delete enquiry or student records after a fixed period. Contact the institute to request a retention review or deletion. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or unresolved matters, and backup copies may remain until replaced.

7. Your requests and choices

Contact the institute email to request access, correction or deletion of information, raise a privacy concern, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Include enough information to identify the relevant record, but do not email passwords. The institute may need to verify your identity. Withdrawal may affect services that require the information and does not remove lawful record-keeping obligations.

8. Children and guardians

If the student is under 18, a parent or lawful guardian should contact the institute about admission and personal information. The institute must arrange any consent and verification required by applicable law. A website checkbox alone is not verification of a guardian. Contact the office if a child's information was submitted without appropriate authorisation.

9. Updates and contact

Updates will be published on this page. Direct privacy requests and complaints to Prashi Tutorial at the institute email, by phone at the institute phone number, or at the institute address.

Website Terms

These terms cover use of the Prashi Tutorial website and student portal. Course-specific admission details and written fee arrangements supplied by the institute also apply to your enrolment.

1. Website information and admissions

Course information, batch availability and schedules may change. Confirm current details with the office before enrolling or making a payment. Sending a form or booking counselling is a request; admission is confirmed only by the institute after its requirements are completed.

2. Fees, cancellations and refunds

Before paying, obtain written confirmation of the course, total fee, instalment dates, inclusions, cancellation conditions and refund terms. This website does not publish a single refund rate or deadline applicable to every course. Do not assume that a refund or batch transfer is automatically available.

For a cancellation, refund, fee discrepancy or transfer request, contact the office with your enrolment and receipt details. The institute will assess the request against your written admission terms and applicable law. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

3. Classes and student conduct

Attend scheduled classes, follow reasonable institute instructions and treat teachers and other students respectfully. Confirm arrangements for missed classes, timetable changes or batch transfers with the office. Harassment, impersonation, cheating and disruptive use of the portal are prohibited.

4. Accounts and passwords

Use only the account issued to you or provided to you as an authorised guardian. Keep credentials confidential, change temporary passwords when prompted and sign out on shared devices. Tell the institute promptly if you suspect misuse. Access may be restricted to protect records or investigate misuse.

5. Learning resources

Notes, questions, answers and other resources are provided for authorised educational use. Do not redistribute, sell or publicly upload institute material without permission from the relevant rights holder. Access depends on your enrolment and what teachers release to your account.

6. Responsible website use

Provide accurate information and submit only material you are entitled to share. Do not upload harmful files, attempt to access another person's records, bypass security checks or interfere with the service. Parents and guardians should contact the office about accounts for students under 18.

7. Academic outcomes and availability

Coaching supports learning but does not guarantee marks, rank, admission or exam success. Past results do not promise an individual outcome. The website or portal may be temporarily unavailable because of maintenance or technical issues; contact the office for time-sensitive academic or fee matters.

8. Privacy and external links

Our Privacy Policy explains how information is handled. External websites have their own terms. The MegaByte credit identifies the website technology provider; course admissions, teaching, fees and student records are managed by Prashi Tutorial.

9. Updates, questions and concerns

Changes to these website terms will be posted here. Changes do not by themselves replace an existing written admission agreement. For questions or complaints, contact contact the institute. Applicable statutory rights remain unaffected.
